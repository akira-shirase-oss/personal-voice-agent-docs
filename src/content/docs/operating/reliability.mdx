title: Reliability and recovery
description: Crashes, restarts, torn writes and stalls — how the service recovers and what it reports.
---

The service assumes it will be killed mid-anything and designs every write for it.

## Crash recovery at open

On start the service scans `ledger/`, verifies checksums, and reports what it found in `ready`'s
`recovered`: `sessions`, `abandoned_turns` (open when the process died), `uncertain_calls` (sent to
a provider, outcome unknown), `torn_tails` (partial writes cut off and quarantined). A torn tail is
never replayed; uncertain calls reconcile as evidence arrives.

```sh run=session
pva_start "$PVA_WORK/data-dir"
pva_send '{"type":"hello","protocol":1,"client":"docs"}'
pva_expect
pva_stop
```

```json output=session
{"type":"ready","protocol":1,"session":"01a1f9a8-b08f-741f-b968-bc870cdb3cb9","consent":{"capture":false,"personal_voice":false},"recovered":{"sessions":0,"abandoned_turns":0,"uncertain_calls":0,"torn_tails":0}}
```

## Liveness and supervision

The app pings the service (`ping` → `pong`, never touching the ledger) and watches for a stall:
a service that stops answering — between turns, before `ready`, mid-turn — is noticed, not left
"starting" forever. On quit the app sends `shutdown`, then SIGTERM at 1 s and SIGKILL at 0.5 s
later, and doesn't quit until the service has exited. A crash storm (50 failed launches in the
suite) stays bounded.

## Durability

Records are appended through a dedicated writer thread, group-committed and fsynced; `flush` is the
honest barrier — `flushed seq` means everything before `seq` is durable. Mode repairs at open are
synced the same way: directories through the directory, files through the file.

## Load evidence

The million-scale runs are committed under `perf/results/` in the product repository with the
machine spec, commit and load average: 1,000,000 durable ledger appends (p99 7.72 ms against a
50 ms budget) and a 1,000,000-event turn run with scripted interruptions and stalls (reply p99
0.41 ms, every expected late result dropped, zero unexpected degradations) on quiet Linux CI —
macOS evidence is labelled separately, and laptop numbers are development observations only.
